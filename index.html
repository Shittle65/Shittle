How to play

  1. A visitor uses the glass toiletThen it flushes. Whatever got flushed is gone and does not count.
  2. Move around to see the amount of shit.Drag to spin, pinch or scroll to zoom. The bowl is glass, so check from the side and below.
  3. Count what stayedEvery piece in the bowl and in the drain. Toys never count.
  4. Guess the weight in 30 secondsSmelly counts 2x, golden 3x, rainbow 5x. Toys count 0.

Drag to look around. Scroll or pinch to zoom. WASD moves.
